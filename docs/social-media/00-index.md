1. **LinkedIn** — highest lead quality; where Operators and L&D buyers spend time

3. **Facebook** — Pakistan-dominant platform; largest local audience
4. **TikTok** — highest organic reach for new accounts; graduate to video by Day 45
5. **Website / Lab Notes** — SEO long game; every post supports organic discovery
6. **Reddit** — authority-building in AI/Python communities; drives referral traffic
7. **Google My Business** — local credibility; maps listing for Pakistan clients

## 3-Phase Overview

### Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1–30)
- Set up all platform profiles with brand-consistent copy and visuals
- Post consistently: LinkedIn 5x/wk, Instagram 4x/wk, Facebook 3x/wk, TikTok 3x/wk, Reddit 3x/wk
- Introduce each content pillar
- First Lab Note published by Day 14
- No paid promotion yet — learn what resonates organically

### Phase 2: Growth (Days 31–60)
- Double down on top-performing content types from Phase 1
- Launch LinkedIn Newsletter
- Introduce Google My Business
- Start WhatsApp Business / Telegram channel
- First experiment with Facebook/Instagram paid boost ($10-20 per best-performing post)
- Second + third Lab Notes drive SEO

### Phase 3: Monetization & Scale (Days 61–100)
- Activate lead generation CTAs consistently
- Drive traffic to Workflow Audit Bot at /lab/audit
- Run workshop promotion campaign on LinkedIn + Facebook
- Monthly analytics review informs content bets
- Begin video content (TikTok screen recordings → YouTube Shorts)
